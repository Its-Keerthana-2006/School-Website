Admissions Open

Green Valley International School welcomes applications from students who aspire to receive quality education in a safe, modern and inspiring learning environment. Admissions are open for Pre-Primary to Grade XII.

Admission Process

  1. Submit Admission Enquiry Form
  2. Collect Application Form
  3. Submit Required Documents
  4. Attend Interaction / Entrance Test
  5. Verification of Documents
  6. Fee Payment
  7. Admission Confirmation
